# Epistemic Pretexting in Language Models

**Pretexting epistémico en modelos de lenguaje**

Preprint by Federico Rodriguez Repetto ([ORCID 0009-0000-2835-5709](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-2835-5709)).

- Page: https://rodriguezrepetto.github.io/epistemic-pretexting/
- DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.24353015](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24353015)
- License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Epistemic pretexting is a form of social engineering in which an unverified statement of identity does not extract restricted content from a language model, but a relaxation of its evidentiary standard.
